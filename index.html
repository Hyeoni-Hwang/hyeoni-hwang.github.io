About

I am a Ph.D student (MS–Ph.D integrated) in Computer Science & Engineering at Korea University (DMIS Lab), advised by Prof. Jaewoo Kang. My research focuses on large language models for biomedical and scientific domains — building pre-training scale corpora for biology, reasoning-focused post-training, and evaluating the reasoning steps of LLMs.

Recently, I worked on LLM mid-training for biological capability at Trillion Labs and LLM post-training for scientific reasoning at SK Telecom.
